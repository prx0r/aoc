# plumber campaign — marketing strategy

## Channels (£0 first)
GBP + weekly posts + local FB groups + realtor ties. Paid only after organic proves saves + DMs. Never split early
spend across platforms.

## Angles (in order)
diagnostic-triage, before_after, scenario.

## Lead proof
repeat servicing revenue.

## Close
DM JOBS £499. One CTA per deck. Founding rate for first 3 (feedback + referral +
case study).

## Measure
Cost per qualified conversation first, cost per customer second.
Rank creatives by leads, never views (`aoc_rank`).

## Compliance
Verify contact source, record basis, TPS/CTPS screen, no auto-marketing to
research records. Personalized variants are research-only until consented
1-to-1 follow-up.
