# garden_familiars campaign — marketing strategy

## Channels (£0 first)

TikTok + Instagram organic (gardentok, planttok, cottage garden). Cross-post
winners. Etsy listing SEO in parallel (separate workstream, same assets).
No paid until an organic creative earns saves + DMs.

## Angles (in order)

character (Sir Hopsalot guards YOUR tomatoes), gift (Boo Bloom dedication),
trust (no IP claims until tested — honesty as differentiator), platform
(one capsule, many creatures), collection (frog + mushroom + ghost).

## Lead proof

Sensor honesty + competition acknowledged in-copy. Never claim validated
costs, test results, waterproof ratings, or Muse microphone streaming.

## Close

DM FROG for launch updates. No price in close (nothing validated).
Price anchors ($39–49 etc.) appear only as labeled hypotheticals inside
decks, never as offers.

## Measure

Saves + DMs + launch-list joins. Separate memory namespace `powthings` —
wait, NO: garden buyers are a third audience. Use namespace `garden`
so plant-lover learnings stay out of both trades AND general POW-Things
content until volumes justify splitting further.
