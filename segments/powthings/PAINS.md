# powthings pains

Research state (design brief), not measured customer outcomes.

1. Beautiful objects with no digital life — plants, jars, instruments, books.
2. Generic smart-home gadgets instead of personal objects.
3. AI assistants blind to the physical things owners love.
4. Gifts that feel mass-produced instead of designed-by-owner.
