# weddings campaign — marketing strategy

## Channels (£0 first)
Instagram + wedding marketplaces. Paid only after organic proves saves + DMs. Never split early
spend across platforms.

## Angles (in order)
qualification, availability, approval-gates.

## Lead proof
incomplete briefs.

## Close
DM WEDDING pilot. One CTA per deck. Founding rate for first 3 (feedback + referral +
case study).

## Measure
Cost per qualified conversation first, cost per customer second.
Rank creatives by leads, never views (`aoc_rank`).

## Compliance
Verify contact source, record basis, TPS/CTPS screen, no auto-marketing to
research records. Personalized variants are research-only until consented
1-to-1 follow-up.
