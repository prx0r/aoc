# HYPOTHESIS — best approach for AI Onboard advertising

## Bet
TikTok **photo-mode slideshows** (native image carousels, not MP4s) are the cheapest
way to test which message makes a UK electrical-business owner start a conversation.
Keep every post manual at first so trending audio can be picked inside TikTok.

## Why slideshows, not video
- £0 to produce at volume with `aoc` (deterministic copy + PIL render).
- Native carousels get swipes = free extra distribution signal.
- Question/indecision hooks ("Which would you automate first?", "Am I doing this wrong?")
  beat declarative product hooks in recent builder reports.
- Video pipeline (`/content` MP4s) is heavier and unnecessary until a message wins.

## The £100 split (unchanged)
- **£0 — organic TikTok + cross-post:** 10–15 slideshows from `aoc`. Publish useful ones
  to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook. Opening slide must identify buyer:
  "UK electricians who own their own business — still doing quotes at 9pm?"
  Then demonstrate one real workflow (WhatsApp enquiry → AI draft → owner approves → quote sent),
  not abstract "AI explained".
- **£70 — Facebook lead test:** one narrow offer × two creatives, with qualification
  question "Do you own/manage a UK trades business?" Facebook reaches older proprietors
  (Markel: 73% of socially-active trades on Facebook); TikTok skews young.
- **£30 — reserve:** extend the winner, or TikTok Promote if UK targeting is available
  in-app. Do NOT split £100 across three paid platforms.

## Account warning (Cambodia → UK)
TikTok ad accounts registered in Cambodia cannot target the UK in self-serve matrix;
UK-registered accounts can. This needs correct business/billing setup, not a VPN.
Organic still works but check audience geography in analytics before promoting.

## What counts as winning
1. Cost per **qualified conversation** with a trades owner (first metric).
2. Cost per **paying £499 customer** (second metric).
3. Log organic enquiries alongside paid. A £10 lead who never buys > £50 lead who buys.
4. Direct calls stay as the £0 control: objections heard live refine the message before spend.

## What AOC does / does not do
- DOES: hook → 5–7 PNGs (1080×1920) → ZIP → human approve → manual TikTok post.
- DOES: track `audience × hook × angle × slides × CTA × style → views/swipes/profile/clicks/leads/sales`,
  mutate winners by **leads**, not views.
- DOES NOT: auto-post, pick trending audio (human does in-app), run paid campaigns.
- DOES NOT: invent stats. Every claim traces to `assets/electrician/proofs.yaml`.

## First batch (10–15)
- 4× opportunity ("still doing quotes at 9pm?" variants)
- 3× before_after (11pm quotes → 2pm quotes)
- 2× faq (replace? hard? tech-savvy?)
- 2× demo (WhatsApp 2pm → quote 2:15pm)
- 2× social_proof (40%/18% gap, 62% missed calls)
Each with 2 hook variants = the mutation pool. Kill losers, mutate winners.
