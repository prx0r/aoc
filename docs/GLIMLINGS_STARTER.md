# GLIMLINGS_STARTER — TikTok ad campaign strategy

## Campaign: `glimlings-starter-01`

**Hypothesis:** Named-character hooks with specific use cases (garden/desk/bedroom)
drive more launch-list DMs than generic creature hooks.

**Offer:** DM GLIMLING for launch updates (waitlist, no purchase).
Price ranges are brand-doc lines (£15-119 by product line), NOT validated offers.

**Budget:** £0 organic. All posting manual. No auto-post tool exists.

---

## 5 formats, 10 hooks, 1 campaign

### Format 1: BEFORE/AFTER (problem → solution)
| Hook | Slide arc |
|------|-----------|
| "Your plant is dying. Mosswick knew — which would you rather have?" | pain → identity → ritual → CTA |
| "Your desk is chaos. Puck filed it — who did you hire?" | pain → identity → ritual → CTA |

**When to use:** Cold traffic. The viewer sees their own problem named,
then the Glimling as the answer. Highest swipe potential.

### Format 2: TRUST (security-first for AI)
| Hook | Slide arc |
|------|-----------|
| "We never ask for passwords. Ever — fair?" | promise → mechanism → approval → CTA |
| "Your data stays yours. Always — agreed?" | promise → mechanism → approval → CTA |

**When to use:** Retargeting or sceptical audiences. AI product = trust
first. The storybook tone disarms the "is this a scam?" reflex.

### Format 3: FAQ (educational, curiosity)
| Hook | Slide arc |
|------|-----------|
| "What actually is a Glimling?" | identity → lines → ritual → CTA |
| "How does a creature manage your alarms?" | mechanism → personality → CTA |

**When to use:** Top-of-funnel. Viewer doesn't know the category yet.
Answers the question they'd ask out loud.

### Format 4: STORY (narrative, social proof)
| Hook | Slide arc |
|------|-----------|
| "I named mine Puck. Now it runs my spreadsheets — yours doesn't?" | personal → mechanism → comparison → CTA |
| "She put Mosswick by the tomatoes. They stopped dying — coincidence?" | personal → mechanism → CTA |

**When to use:** Mid-funnel. The viewer knows what a Glimling is;
now they need to see someone using one. First-person = native TikTok.

### Format 5: COMPARISON (vs alternatives)
| Hook | Slide arc |
|------|-----------|
| "Plastic robot toys vs Glimlings — which one glows?" | contrast → identity → CTA |
| "Smart speaker vs Glimling — which one has a name?" | contrast → identity → CTA |

**When to use:** Bottom-of-funnel. Viewer is deciding between options.
Comparison = category creation. "Glimling" is the new noun.

## Posting strategy

### Week 1: establish the category
- **Day 1:** FAQ "What actually is a Glimling?" (educate)
- **Day 3:** Before/After "Your plant is dying" (pain + solution)
- **Day 5:** Trust "We never ask for passwords" (disarm scepticism)

### Week 2: social proof + comparison
- **Day 8:** Story "I named mine Puck" (personal use case)
- **Day 10:** Comparison "Plastic robot vs Glimling" (category creation)
- **Day 12:** Before/After "Your desk is chaos" (different segment)

### Week 3: iterate from data
- Read `aoc_rank` by leads (not views).
- Kill hooks below 25% hook rate at 48h.
- Mutate top-2: same angle, new hook wording.

###Cross-posting
- TikTok Photo Mode (swipeable carousels) — primary.
- Instagram Reels (same ZIP, different caption) — secondary.
- Facebook (best performers only, £70 test budget when ready).

## Measurement

Primary metric: **DMs with keyword GLIMLING** (trackable via launch list).
Secondary: saves per view (TikTok algorithm signal).
Tertiary: profile visits → website clicks.

Funnel: DM → launch list → quickstart offer (£20) → standard (£499).
All manual. No auto-DM tool exists (and none should).
