# FREE PLAYBOOK — £0 marketing per vertical (no ad spend)

Consensus across 2026 trade-marketing sources: Google Business Profile +
reviews + one community channel beats everything else at £0. Paid accelerates;
it never substitutes.

## Universal £0 foundation (every vertical, week 1)

1. **Google Business Profile, completed** — every field, exact trade category,
   service area, phone, hours, 10+ real-work photos. Highest-ROI free action.
2. **5 reviews via SMS at payment** — text a direct review link within 2–4h of
   the job. Compounds: reviews → rank → calls → more reviews.
3. **WhatsApp Business app** (free tier, not Cloud API) — professional profile,
   after-hours auto-reply. Costs nothing.
4. **Referral habit** — ask every happy customer; stamp-card for repeat trades.
5. **Free directories** — Yell, FreeIndex, TrustATrader, scheme finders
   (NICEIC/NAPIT for sparkies). Citations + direct leads.

## Electrician (£0)

- GBP services listed individually: EICR, consumer unit, EV charger, rewire,
  emergency callouts. Secondary categories: Electrical Installation, EV Charging.
- NICEIC/NAPIT contractor search profiles (free for members, high-trust leads).
- `aoc` slideshows → TikTok organic + cross-post. 10–15 to start.
- Local Facebook groups: join 5–10 area groups, answer electrical questions
  genuinely, respond to "looking for electrician" posts from personal profile.
  Never spam or cold-DM — admins ban it and it burns trust.
- First enquiry in 4–8 weeks is realistic once GBP + 5 reviews land.

## Nails / beauty (£0)

- GBP is the acquisition channel ("nail tech near me" = intent to book):
  category "Nail salon"/"Nail technician", every service + price, booking link,
  2–3 fresh work photos/month, review text ~1h post-appointment.
- Instagram is the **portfolio**, not acquisition: 2–3 best sets/week, location
  in captions ("gel nails Manchester"), booking link in bio. Keeps existing
  clients, shows work to the curious.
- Free booking tool (Fresha/Booksy/Calendly/Cal.com) linked in bio + GBP.
  Evening/weekend impulse bookings die without it.
- Stamp-card loyalty (physical card works): every 5th half-price or free
  treatment after 8 visits. Mention at first appointment.
- Wedding/event partnerships: local planners, venues, photographers — occasion
  work in exchange for referrals.
- Facebook groups (nail/tech + local area): your guess is right, with the same
  rule — genuine help, recommendation-request replies, no spam. Nail-tech peer
  groups are for learning/suppliers, not clients; local community groups are
  where clients ask.

## Plumber (£0)

- GBP with every service listed separately (drain, water heater, leak, sewer —
  Google matches queries to listed services). Weekly GBP post, Thu/Fri 9–11am.
- Review text 2–4h post-job; QR on invoice. 12→87 reviews in 4 months is a
  documented pattern; calls roughly triple.
- Local Facebook groups (5–15): genuine presence, answer questions, respond to
  "looking for a plumber" from personal profile. Highest-converting £0 leads.
- Realtor / property-manager / inspector relationships: 2–5 referrals/month
  each once active; close 60–80% on pre-built trust.
- Nextdoor/local equivalents: 15 min/day answering local questions.

## Sole trader, any trade (£0)

- GBP + 5 reviews + WhatsApp Business + one free directory listing. That's the
  starter kit. Website can wait until enquiries flow; GBP covers Maps + many
  "near me" searches alone.
- `aoc` sole_trader slides: findable + bookable + paid angle.

## What £0 can't do (honest)

- Speed: organic takes 4–8 weeks to first enquiry, 3–6 months to compound.
- Emergency trades in competitive cities will still lose some jobs to advertisers.
- Reviews require asking every time — the system fails the week you stop asking.

## Our slideshows: honest quality grade

- Copy/structure: B+. Buyer-first hooks, one idea per slide, proof before CTA,
  word limits enforced by gates. Matches every structure research recommends.
- Visuals: C. Solid colors, no photography, no faces. Research says plain
  high-contrast is acceptable and faceless works — but real work photos
  (fuse boards, nail sets, boiler installs, vans) would lift swipe-through.
  Free fix: shoot on a phone, pass as `backgrounds` to the renderer.
  Photo library pattern documented, not yet wired.
- Overall: postable now, improvable free. Content quality is not the blocker;
  volume + measurement is.
