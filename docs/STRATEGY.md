# STRATEGY — source notes for the £100 experiment (distilled from briefing)

|---|---|---|

## Account country warning
Cambodia-registered self-serve accounts cannot target UK; UK-registered can.
Needs correct business/billing setup, not VPN. Organic works — check analytics
geography before promoting.

## Where trades are (Markel Direct, Jul 2026, n=501 socially-active)
- Facebook 73%, Instagram 63% for business. TikTok 73% only in 18–24 slice (not overall).
- FB worth paid test (older proprietors = buyers). TikTok = younger sparkies + apprentices/hobbyists noise.
- Indicative costs (other advertisers, not AIO): FB clicks £0.30–1.50; FB leads £15–40; Google leads £35–75.
- Rule: £10 lead who never buys > £50 lead who buys £499.

## £100 split
- £0: 10–15 slideshows via AOC, cross-post TikTok/IG/FB.
- £70: one narrow FB lead offer × 2 creatives + qualification Q (own/manage UK trades business?).
- £30: extend winner or TikTok Promote if UK targeting available.
- Don't split £100 three ways — too thin for evidence.

## Creative rule
Slide 1 identifies buyer: "UK electricians who own their own business — still doing quotes at 9pm?"
Then real workflow demo, not abstract AI. Track cost/qualified-conversation, then cost/customer.
Direct calls = £0 control + live objections.
